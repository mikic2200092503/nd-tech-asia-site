The Material

What this resin is, and what it isn't

Ion exchange resin swaps dissolved ions in water for others of the same charge — used to soften water, remove salts, or strip out contaminants. Mixed-bed resin blends cation and anion beads for high-purity "polishing", most often in semiconductor, power and pharmaceutical systems.

Both lots here were originally built for exactly that kind of high-purity duty. They've been used and taken out of their original system, and we haven't regenerated or reprocessed either one — what you see is what you get, and you're welcome to verify it however you like.

Where This Type Is Used

What mixed-bed resin is normally used for

Built for high-purity polishing, not basic softening — the right fit depends on the purity level you need.

Semiconductor ultrapure water

Final polishing to remove trace ions, silica and TOC before water touches wafers.

Power generation

Boiler and turbine feedwater demineralization, protecting against scale and corrosion.

Pharmaceutical & lab water

High-purity water for pharma processing and lab-grade applications.

Demineralization / desalting

Removing nearly all dissolved salts — the core job a mixed bed does.

General industrial polishing

Final ion polishing after reverse osmosis, where filtration alone isn't enough.
